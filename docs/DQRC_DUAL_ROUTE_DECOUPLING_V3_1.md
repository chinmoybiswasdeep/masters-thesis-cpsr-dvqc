## The decisive finding

**V3's primary nonlinearity metric was pinned exactly at its own ceiling, and that -- not the physics -- is the main reason its `(g,J)` surface looked flat.**

V3 reported `NL0_raw = 2.000` at `max_degree = 3`. The number of single-delay `tau=0` targets at that setting is exactly 2 (`L2`, `L3`), each capped at capacity 1.0. The metric was therefore at **100% of its target-count ceiling**. A saturated metric has zero gradient by construction: it cannot respond to `(g,J)`, to `m`, or to anything else.

Measured directly (same processor, same seeds, `T = 200`):

| `max_degree` | `NL0_raw` | available targets | saturated? | relative `(g,J)` dynamic range `E_NL` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 (V3's setting) | 2.0000 | 2 | **yes, exactly** | **0.044** |
| 5 | 4.0000 | 4 | **yes, exactly** | -- |
| 8 | 5.0863 | 7 | no | **0.222** |

Raising the order horizon until the metric has headroom increases the relative `(g,J)` dynamic range **five-fold**, from 0.044 to 0.222 -- crossing the preregistered `E_NL > 0.20` discovery gate. The `(g,J)` structure is physically sensible once visible: `NL_0` rises from 3.245 at weak interactions (`g=0.2, J=0.1`) to ~4.14 at stronger ones.

This does **not** establish that the processor is controllable -- that requires discovery and confirmation at full scale. It establishes that V3's negative controllability evidence was substantially an artifact and cannot be relied upon.

### The response-angle correction

V3 declared the geometry `NOT EVALUABLE` whenever a response vector had zero norm -- which happens precisely when decoupling is perfect. V3.1 instead computes

```
v_m   = (dM/dm, dNL/dm)
q_hat = grad_{g,J} NL / ||grad_{g,J} NL||
v_P   = (grad_{g,J} M . q_hat,  grad_{g,J} NL . q_hat)
```

and the angle between them. Ideal decoupling now returns **exactly 90 degrees** (asserted in `tests/test_v3_1_science.py::test_ideal_decoupling_gives_ninety_degrees_not_not_evaluable`); `NOT EVALUABLE` is reserved for a *whole* vector being unresolved.

## Processor and memory variants

**Processors** (`processor_variants.py`): `P0` (V3 baseline), `P1` (interleaved data re-uploading, encoder axis alternating Z/X between interacting layers), `P2` (multi-axis simultaneous encoding), `P3` (multi-tap, each delayed input on its own qubit so cross-delay terms can only come from the interaction). Verified numerically: `[V_k, H_0] != 0` for all variants; `[V_k, V_l] != 0` for `P1`/`P3` (5.600).

**Ablation ladder**: `encoding_only` (`g=J=0`), `two_body_only`, `four_body_only`, `commuting`, `noninteracting`, `full`, `randomized`.

**Memories** (`memory_variants.py`): `M0` (V3's fractional SWAP) and `M1` (explicit CPTP retention, `E_m(rho) = m rho + (1-m)|0><0|`, verified trace-preserving at every `m`).

An audit of `M0` explains V3's coarse-scan/local-derivative contradiction: `SWAP^m = P_+ + e^{i pi m} P_-` moves **transfer probability and singlet phase together** (transfer 0.206 -> 1.000 while the phase runs 0.94 -> 3.14 rad as `m` goes 0.3 -> 0.9). `m` in `M0` is therefore not a pure retention knob, which is exactly why a monotone coarse scan can coexist with a negative local slope.

### An honest negative result about `M1`

`M1` gives `m` an unambiguous retention meaning -- but under **exact** expectation-value readout its memory capacity is essentially flat in `m`:

| `m` | `M_signed` (exact readout) | `M_signed` (512 shots) |
|---|---|---|
| 0.2 | +3.368 | +2.396 |
| 0.6 | +3.312 | +3.545 |
| 1.0 | +3.422 | +3.672 |

A pure contraction is invertible, and ridge capacity is invariant under invertible rescaling, so noiseless IPC cannot see retention loss. (This reproduces a finding already recorded from an earlier phase of this project.) **Consequence: "memory controllability" in this metric is not about retention at all under noiseless readout** -- `M0`'s apparent control came from the phase/transport change. Genuine retention control requires finite-shot readout, which is why shot noise is reported alongside. This is a limitation of the metric, not evidence that either memory is broken.

## Decision gate: does `E_NL > 0.20` hold across seeds at `max_degree = 8`?

Full discovery as configured costs **20 hours** (972 evaluations × 74 s, measured). Rather than spend that blindly, the one question that determines whether it is worth running was answered first: does the unsaturated `(g,J)` dynamic range survive across independent seeds?

Script: `code/run_v3_1_enl_check.py`. Data: `results/dqrc_dual_route_v3_1/e_nl_cross_seed_check.json`. Estimator settings **identical** to the notebook's DISCOVERY stage (`T=420`, `max_delay=8`, `max_degree=8`, `max_targets_per_degree=30`, `n_surrogates=49`, `N_P=4`, `R=3`); only the `(g,J)` grid is coarser (3×3), a recorded resource decision, not a protocol change. Discovery seeds only. 54 evaluations, 78.7 min.

| processor | `E_NL` per seed (−301, −302, −303) | median | gate `>0.20` | ceiling-contaminated |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `P0` (V3 baseline) | 0.183, 0.194, **0.204** | **0.194** | **1 / 3** | 0 / 27 |
| `P1` (re-uploading) | 0.009, 0.018, 0.021 | **0.018** | **0 / 3** | 0 / 27 |

`NL_0` hierarchical CI: `P0` mean 4.601 [4.413, 4.790] (between-reservoir var 0.017, within 0.164); `P1` mean 4.354 [4.108, 4.488] (between 0.046, within 0.001).

Three things matter here:

1. **The measurement is clean.** Ceiling fractions span 0.72–0.90 against a 0.95 threshold, so **zero** evaluations are ceiling-contaminated, and null fractions are 0.129–0.202. This is *not* a repeat of the V3 artifact: the metric had real headroom and still shows only marginal control authority. The ceiling repair worked, and the answer it revealed is a genuine physical one.
2. **The motivating single-seed number was optimistic.** `E_NL = 0.222` came from one seed; across three it is 0.183–0.204 with median 0.194, sitting just *below* the preregistered gate. The gate was frozen before this run and is **not** moved to accommodate the result.
3. **The re-uploading design hypothesis is refuted.** `P1` was built expecting *more* `(g,J)` authority from interleaving the encoding between interacting layers. It delivers roughly **ten times less** (median 0.018 vs 0.194): its per-layer encoding generates so much nonlinearity that the interaction contributes almost nothing on top, which is consistent with the SMOKE ablation finding that encoding dominates. Reported as a failed design, not omitted.

**Consequence:** no processor variant clears the preregistered controllability gate on all seeds, so no candidate may advance. The 20-hour discovery sweep and the held-out confirmation were **not** run -- spending that budget to confirm a candidate that already fails its screening gate would be wasteful and scientifically improper.

## Conclusion: Outcome B (structurally separated, processor control weak)

Claim 1 (structural separation) is **established** and is exact: `X_P` is bit-identically invariant to `m` and `X_M` to `(g,J)`, verified over many random configurations, with an injected-dependency control that fails as required.

Claim 2 (functional controllability) is **not established, and the discovery-stage evidence is against it**: on genuinely unsaturated data the best processor reaches a median `E_NL` of 0.194 against a preregistered 0.20 gate, passing on only 1 of 3 seeds. Claim 3 (matched-resource usefulness) is **not established** -- it was never reached, because no candidate advanced.

This is Outcome **B**: the architecture delivers exactly the structural separation it was designed for, but the processor's `(g,J)` handle is too weak to steer instantaneous nonlinearity reliably. Structural independence is real; functional controllability is not demonstrated.

| level | claim | status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | software-valid modular architecture | supported |
| 2 | exact structural branch isolation | supported |
| 3 | nontrivial independent memory and nonlinear modules | supported |
| 4 | robust functional control decoupling | **not supported** |
| 5 | matched-resource Pareto improvement | not supported |

**Highest supported claim level: 3.**

The decision gate settled the open question: `E_NL` did **not** hold across seeds, so the honest outcome is **B**, not a confirmed Claim 2. Level 4 would require a processor whose interaction term actually steers `NL_0`, which this family does not provide.

### Recommended next step

Do **not** re-run discovery on this processor family. The binding constraint is control authority, not measurement: the metric is unsaturated (ceiling 0.72–0.90) and the null bias is acceptable (0.13–0.20), yet `(g,J)` still moves `NL_0` by under 20%. Two directions follow from the evidence rather than from hope:

1. **Reduce the encoding's share of the nonlinearity** so the interaction has something left to control -- weaker encoder amplitude (`alpha`, `beta`), fewer re-uploading layers, or a deliberately near-linear encoder. `P1`'s failure is informative here: more encoding made control *worse*, so the productive direction is the opposite one.
2. **Give `(g,J)` a longer lever** -- more microsteps `R` at fixed encoding strength, so the interaction acts for longer between injections, or a larger `N_P` where the four-body term is not near-degenerate.

Both are cheap to screen with the same `run_v3_1_enl_check.py` gate before any full sweep is contemplated.

## Limitations

* Only SMOKE executed. Discovery and confirmation are implemented and gated but unrun.
* 4 of the 30 requested figures were produced; the remaining 26 (seed-level confirmation, neighborhood map, Pareto frontier, hypervolume bootstrap, frozen-vs-retrained readout, back-action trajectories, per-variant `NL(g,J)` surfaces) require the unexecuted stages and were **not** fabricated.
* At SMOKE scale the null fraction is ~0.50, far above the 0.20 gate -- 43 training samples is scientifically underdetermined, which is exactly why SMOKE carries no claim.
* `N_P = 3` in SMOKE makes the four-body term identically zero, so `four_body_only`, `commuting` and `noninteracting` coincide.
* The confirmation body is scaffolded (seed handling, freeze verification, gate wiring) but its measurement loop is not filled in; it is reported as unexecuted rather than as producing results.
* `M1` retention is invisible to noiseless IPC (see above); finite-shot readout is required for genuine retention control.
